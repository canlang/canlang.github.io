Featured Research & Systems

Ph.D. Dissertation • IEEE Access '20

Augmented 3D Geomagnetic Indoor Positioning

Infrastructure-free indoor positioning leveraging ambient geomagnetic anomalies. Formulates augmented gradient vectors and sequence matching to achieve orientation-invariant sub-meter positioning without external beacon deployment.

  • Eliminates magnetic heading sensitivity through multi-axis augmented vector modeling.
  • Maintains meter-level accuracy in complex reinforced concrete structures without anchor installation.
Paper (IEEE Access) → Dissertation →
MDPI Sensors '20 • ACM MobiSys '19

Autonomous Mobile Robot SLAM & Radio Map Construction

Autonomous robotic survey systems ('Rover') fusing LiDAR/inertial SLAM with Wi-Fi positioning. Automatically constructs dense indoor radio maps with centimeter-grade odometry, replacing manual fingerprinting site surveys.

  • Pose-graph optimization coupling Wi-Fi signal decay with mobile robot odometry and loop-closure.
  • Demonstrated autonomous radio map construction at ACM MobiSys '19 in Seoul.
Paper (MDPI Sensors) → MobiSys '19 →
Platform • Pervasive & Mobile Computing '16

KAILOS: Global Crowdsourced Indoor Positioning Platform

Pioneered crowdsourced indoor positioning and navigation systems. Built cloud-native log ingestion pipelines, floor-plan registration tools, and client positioning SDKs serving real-world users.

  • Crowdsourcing framework aggregating heterogeneous smartphone sensor logs into coherent global radio maps.
  • Deployed as a live public positioning platform at kailos.io and evaluated in large commercial complexes.
